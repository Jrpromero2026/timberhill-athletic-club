03

You do not need a reason we would recognise.

Most people who start training here arrive with something ordinary. One of these is usually close enough.

  • 01You have not trained in years and the gym floor feels like someone else's territory.
  • 02You train regularly and nothing has changed in six months.
  • 03You are coming back from an injury and are not sure what is safe.
  • 04You want to get stronger and every program you read contradicts the last one.
  • 05You have an event with a date on it and a distance you have never covered.
  • 06You are in your sixties or seventies and want to stay independent.
  • 07You are pregnant or postpartum and need a program built for that, not adjusted for it.
  • 08Your teenager wants to lift and you want it taught properly the first time.
  • 09Your doctor told you to exercise without telling you which exercise.
  • 10You are in season and cannot afford to break down before it ends.
  • 11You take the classes and want something built around you instead.
  • 12You know what to do. You just do not do it unless someone is expecting you.
  • 13You have lost weight before and would like it to be the last time.
04

What personal training actually is

ASSESS
Movement, training history, and what you actually want out of this — before anything is prescribed.
PLAN
A written program with a starting point and a direction, not a workout picked on the day.
COACH
Sessions on the floor with someone watching the reps and correcting them.
PROGRESS
Load, volume and difficulty move on a schedule, so the work keeps asking something of you.
ADAPT
Travel, illness, a bad week. The plan changes. The direction does not.
MEASURE
Numbers you can see, reviewed with you, so progress is not a matter of opinion.
05

How it works

  1. 01

    Book a complimentary consultation

    Thirty minutes, free, no commitment. Pick a time that works — the booking page opens straight on available slots.

  2. 02

    Talk it through

    Goals, history, schedule, injuries, and what has and has not worked for you before. Mostly we listen.

  3. 03

    We match you to a trainer

    You do not have to choose one from a page of photographs. We put you with the trainer whose specialty fits what you described.

  4. 04

    First session

    An assessment and the first version of your program, so you leave knowing exactly what you are doing next week.

  5. 05

    Train, review, adjust

    Sessions at whatever frequency you agreed, with progress reviewed on a set cadence rather than whenever it comes up.
